# Test
for this playtest, we want players to interact with the shopkeeper and edit their deck. To do this the player must:
1. Use WASD to move around in the 3d world and navigate to the deck editor
2. look at their current deck and edit as they see fit
3. navigate to the shopkeeper
4. Interact with the shopkeeper using
5. buy a card in the shop
6. go back to the deck editor and add their new card to the deck

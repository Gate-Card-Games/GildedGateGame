# GildedGateGame
-----------------------------------------------------------
# SCOPE

## MUST
- Create the base mechanics of the open world, battle system, and deck system
- have a win and lose mechanic
- create things to interact with in the world
- create the art for everything
- make a few simple cards
## SHOULD
- add more than a few cards
- make the battle system feel better
- make the cards more in depth
- make the story elements
- sound effects
## COULD
- add even more cards
- make music for the game
- sound effects
-----------------------------------------------------------
# RISKS
1. Development Scope - We have a decently large scope, and as such is a risk with how much time we have, this will be tested by the final project, if this fails we will re-evaluate scope and 
2. Art Direction - None of us are artists, and as such we have a large part of the game that is not accounted for in our skill sets, this will be tested by the models and art we make, if this fails we will use placeholders for the art.
3. Feature Creep - We have many ideas

# TEAM AGREEMENT
Our team will use Trello (linked here:https://trello.com/b/fLj5G2ij/gpr-100-group-assignment) to keep track of our project progress, as well as communicate over IMessage in a Group Chat, Have a Merge rule of peer reviewing each commit, and have a weekly meeting of Monday at 2:30. If someone misses a task, we will have them finish it as soon as possible otherwise they will be forced to take on more work, and the other members of the team will pick up the slack.
